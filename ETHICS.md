# Ethics & Rules · Anshaxa iyo Xeerarka

Everything we teach can cause real harm if misused. Membership depends on following these rules.

**1. Only test what you are allowed to test.**
Written permission, an official bug-bounty scope, or a lab built for practice. "I was only looking" is not a defence.
*Kaliya tijaabi waxa laguu oggol yahay: oggolaansho qoran, scope bug-bounty, ama lab loogu talagalay.*

**2. Respect scope.** Out-of-scope domains, social engineering and denial of service are off-limits unless the program explicitly allows them.

**3. Do no damage.** Don't delete, modify or exfiltrate more data than needed to prove impact. Stop and report.

**4. Report responsibly.** Tell the owner first, privately, and give them reasonable time to fix it before any public disclosure.

**5. Never attack Somali institutions, businesses or people "to prove a point."** If you find a vulnerability in a local organisation without a disclosure program, bring it to the community leads and we will help you report it safely.

**6. No sharing of stolen data, credentials or malware** in the community, not even "for learning".

**7. Know the law.** Unauthorised access is a crime in Somalia, Kenya and almost every country. You are responsible for your own actions.

Breaking these rules means removal from the community.
