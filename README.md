## Who we are · Annaga

**EN:** Somali Red Team is a free, Discord-based community founded in January 2025. We help young people in Somalia and the diaspora go from zero to job-ready in offensive security: how attackers think, how to test systems legally, and how to report what you find.

**SO:** Somali Red Team waa bulsho bilaash ah oo ka shaqaysa Discord, la aasaasay Janaayo 2025. Waxaan dhallinyarada Soomaaliyeed u barnaa amniga weerarka (offensive security): sida uu u fekero haakarku, sida si sharci ah loo tijaabiyo nidaamyada, iyo sida loo soo sheego dhibaatooyinka la helo.

## Start here · Halkan ka bilow

1. Read **[ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md)** (non-negotiable).
2. Follow **[the roadmap](roadmap/README.md)** from Stage 0.
3. Practise on the **[legal labs](labs/README.md)** only.
4. Publish a **[writeup](writeups/TEMPLATE.md)** for every box you finish. It's your portfolio.
5. Join the Discord and ask questions. Nobody is too much of a beginner.
